How to watch

Four things worth knowing.

Find something
Scroll the rows on Home, or tap Search at the bottom to find a specific title.
Watch it
Open a title and tap Play. For a series, pick an episode first.
If it won't load
Tap the source name at the top and switch to another source — some go down temporarily.
Save for offline
On a title, tap Download — watch it later under Downloads.

Want your list and history on every device? Settings → Connections to sign in. Optional — everything works without an account.

Troubleshooting
Stuck? Quick fixes

The things people hit most.

Usually an old copy is in the way, or the download was incomplete. Uninstall any old Zangetsu, re-download the APK fully, and pick arm64 (or universal if unsure).

Tap the source name at the top and switch to another source, or pick a different server on the episode. If a whole source looks dead, check Settings → Source health, or add another source.

Check your internet and tap Try Again. Some ISPs block GitHub — turn on Settings → DNS (Cloudflare) and retry. You can also tap Skip for now and add sources later.
